# Grant review precision

A comparative study of how precisely reviewers can distinguish grant proposals at different positions on a rating scale. The planned outputs are two comparative figures, a study design and results table, a short article for Jorge Piano Simões's website, and a reproducibility package for Zenodo.

## Data

The [NIH R01 sample](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/ShinyItemAnalysis/html/NIH.html) and [AIBS biomedical reviews](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.12728087) include criterion scores and overall ratings. The [SNSF case studies](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4531160) supply individual panel votes. Source data and derived review records are recreated locally from public sources.

## Study status

The first stage audits reviewer assignments, available criteria, review stages and comparison groups before model fitting. The question of whether any dataset supports a stable upper cutoff is open.
